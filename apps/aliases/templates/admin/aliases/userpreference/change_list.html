Why is the donate preference stored server-side?

The donation decision is made inside POST /api/aliases/categorize-all/ on the backend. Keeping it here means:

  1. The preference survives client reinstalls and SharedPreferences wipes.
  2. A malicious client cannot forge donations by flipping the flag per-request.
  3. The decision lives in one place — the backend — not duplicated across clients.

The Flutter app mirrors this value in SharedPreferences for instant UI reads and syncs changes via PATCH /api/aliases/preferences/.
